Littlehouse

A tiny dollhouse life sim

  • Four residents live here on their own. Their faces show their mood and a thought bubble says what they want.
  • Tap someone to see their needs and queue things for them to do.
  • Three of them go out to work by day and bring home pay. Spend it in Buy: pick an item, then tap a spot in a room.
  • No bed means a night on the floor, grumpily. No shower means nobody gets clean.
  • Bills arrive at midnight. Aim for happy days: a day ending with the household's mood at 60 or more.

Keys: Space pause · 1 2 3 speed · R next resident · F next furniture · P B H tabs · arrows and Enter to place · X cancel · M mute
