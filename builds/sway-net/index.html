The knobs

Softness is the compliance, in real units: 0 is a rigid rod, the top of the range is a rubber band. Substeps is solver quality, and it is not an innocent knob. At 8 substeps a ten link chain hangs about 70 percent stretchier than the material you asked for, because one solver sweep only carries news one link along the rope. Turn substeps up and the fabric visibly firms up toward the number the softness slider actually promised. Press Measure to watch that tested against statics, live.
