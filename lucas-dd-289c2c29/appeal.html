Disagree with a decision

Use this page if Lucas DD turned down a request for a service, cut it, paused it or stopped it, or made an eligibility decision you disagree with. You can also use it for a complaint about how things are done. It works out your time limits and writes a letter you send yourself.

This is a family's own planning tool. It is not the Lucas County Board of Developmental Disabilities, is not connected to it, and does not decide eligibility. Only the board's trained staff decide.

Before you start

  • It helps to have the letter from Lucas DD nearby, and the date it reached you.
  • You have 90 days from the letter to appeal. To keep a service while you appeal, act before the day the change starts.
  • You can have someone help you at any time, like a friend or an advocacy group. You can skip anything, or choose unsure.

Nothing you type leaves this device. The page saves nothing. Close it and it is gone.
